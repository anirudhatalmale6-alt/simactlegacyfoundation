About the Foundation

Haiti’s greatest asset is its people.

Our broader vision is to strengthen communities and expand opportunities for the Haitian people, helping build a Haiti where every generation can live, work, prosper, and create a meaningful future at home.

In partnership with SImACT and other stakeholders, the Foundation develops and supports initiatives in preventive healthcare, education, humanitarian assistance, and other critical sectors where sustainable opportunities are needed most. We measure our success not simply by the assistance we provide, but by the lasting progress, opportunity and improved quality of life our work helps create.

  • People FirstHuman dignity, opportunity and community well-being at the centre of the work.
  • PartnershipInvestors, entrepreneurs, institutions, communities and development partners, brought together.
  • Responsible InvestmentOpportunities that create economic value while weighing their social and environmental impact.
  • Sustainable DevelopmentSolutions that create lasting value rather than short-term results.
  • Integrity & AccountabilityTransparency and accountability, because trust is fundamental to lasting partnerships.
  • Long-Term ImpactSuccess measured by the progress our work helps create for generations to come.
Our Projects

Making a difference,
one initiative at a time.

Through targeted initiatives and community partnerships, the Foundation works to address essential needs while creating pathways toward greater opportunity, dignity, and long-term community well-being.

01

Clean Water

Access to safe, reliable and clean water for communities in Haiti’s southern and northern departments — improving public health, strengthening community well-being, and setting a basic foundation for dignity and sustainable development.

02

Scholarships

Educational support for elementary school-age children, helping families overcome barriers to education and giving children greater opportunities to learn, grow, and build a brighter future.

03

Housing Assistance

A special housing programme supporting families facing housing challenges through compassionate, community-centred assistance that promotes stability, dignity, and improved living conditions.

From Vision to Action

We identify the critical need,
then bring the right partners to it.

Working in partnership with SImACT, local organisations, communities, public institutions and other stakeholders, we develop and support initiatives designed to address immediate needs while building long-term capacity and opportunity.

01Investing in PeopleImproving access to healthcare, education, skills and economic opportunity, with particular attention to children, families and underserved communities.
02Strengthening CommunitiesHousing, infrastructure, community development and other initiatives that contribute to healthier, safer and more resilient communities.
03Creating Sustainable OpportunityProjects that move beyond short-term assistance to create pathways for employment, entrepreneurship, education and economic participation.
04Building PartnershipsBringing together the Company, donors, investors, nonprofits, government institutions, local leaders and communities to leverage resources and expertise.
05Supporting Prevention and ResiliencePrioritising preventive healthcare, education, preparedness and other approaches that address challenges before they become crises.

Through these actions the Foundation seeks to transform philanthropy into opportunity, assistance into empowerment, and individual initiatives into lasting community impact.

Accomplishments & Impact

A legacy of service and opportunity.

Through more than a decade of service, the Foundation has sought to transform philanthropic contributions into tangible and lasting opportunities — across education, health, sports, infrastructure, culture and human development.

Community development
and infrastructure

Our infrastructure initiatives have included support for the construction and improvement of road, including the main road connecting the National Highway to the Belle Rive site. These investments have contributed to improved connectivity and accessibility, while supporting the broader economic and social development of the surrounding community in the Greater Jacmel area.

The Foundation also supports cultural activities and local talent, creating opportunities for individuals to express themselves, develop their abilities and contribute to the cultural vitality of their communities.

Coming Next

The SLF
Training Center

A forward-looking initiative designed to expand access to trade, vocational and technology training for deserving individuals within the local community.

The Training Center will focus on practical, market-relevant skills that create pathways to employment, entrepreneurship and greater economic independence. By providing individuals with opportunities to acquire technical and technological competencies, the initiative seeks to address both individual aspirations and broader workforce-development needs within the community.

It represents the next chapter in the Foundation’s commitment to sustainable development — moving beyond providing assistance toward creating lasting pathways to self-sufficiency and economic opportunity.
